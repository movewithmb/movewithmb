Common questions

DVA physio FAQs

Do I need a referral for DVA physiotherapy?

Gold Card holders don't need a referral. White Card holders need a valid referral from their GP for the specific accepted condition being treated.

Is there a cost to me?

No, physiotherapy does not cost you anything out of pocket if you hold a Department of Veterans' Affairs (DVA) Card]

How many sessions am I entitled to?

This depends on your individual treatment plan and condition. We'll discuss what's appropriate for your recovery at your first appointment.

Can I book an evening or Saturday appointment?

Yes — we offer Monday to Friday evening appointments and Saturday morning slots.
